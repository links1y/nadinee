To My Dearest Sister, Nadine... ♡

I wanted to write these words straight from my heart, as a constant reminder of how deeply precious you are to me. 🤍

Never let unkind words or anyone’s lack of appreciation make you second-guess your value. You are an extraordinary soul—so beautiful, genuinely kind, and endlessly graceful. If someone failed to see your true worth, it reflects only their blindness, never your value.

Please never allow anyone to make you doubt yourself or feel undeserving of life's sweetest blessings. You deserve all the beauty, happiness, and peace this world has to offer—and so much more.

I want you to always remember that I am far more than just a brother to you; I am your forever safe haven. Whenever life gets heavy, you never have to explain or say much—just know I will always be right here, holding your hand through it all.

I love you endlessly, and nothing brings me more joy than seeing you radiant, peaceful, and fiercely confident in who you are. ✨
